## Projects

- **[SideQuest](https://mysidequest.app)**: an AI game master that turns your neighbourhood into a mystery quest for you and your friends.
- **ByeByeScammer**: an iOS app backed by an AI agent that acts as your bodyguard on phone calls. It spots scams in real time and steps in before you get tricked.
- **Bedrock**: point it at a product and it traces who really owns it, with a source for every fact.
